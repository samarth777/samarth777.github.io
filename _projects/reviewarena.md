## Overview

ReviewArena is a large-scale resource for studying automated scientific peer review. It brings together **51,529 papers** and **196,099 reviews** from seven OpenReview venues, preserving native review forms, scores, rebuttals, meta-reviews, decisions, and paper text.

## ReviewArena-Eval

The project also introduces a 1,002-paper evaluation benchmark aligned to the scoring conventions of six conferences. Experiments with six open-weight language models show persistent problems with calibration, rating-scale compression, and accept/reject discrimination.
