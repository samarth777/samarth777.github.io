## Overview

EarthInfer is an agentic framework for answering complex questions about environmental risk and land analysis from satellite imagery. It combines TerraMind with autonomous Earth-observation data retrieval and spatiotemporal reasoning.

Rather than relying only on the original image, the system generates task-relevant geospatial modalities such as vegetation indices and land-use or land-cover maps during reasoning.
